Profile

I am a Public Administration student at Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP) with an interest in governance, public policy, human rights, social protection and digital transformation in the public sector. I also enjoy writing, research and youth development activities.

Research Interests

Public Administration · Governance · Public Policy · Human Rights · Social Protection · Digital Government · Public-Private Partnerships

Selected Research Projects

Digital Transformation and Organizational Change in Bangladesh’s Public Sector: A qualitative study of E-Porcha implementation in land offices.

Law Enforcement and Human Rights in Bangladesh: A study of developments across two political periods (2009–2024 and post-August 2024).

Women’s Participation in Social Protection Programs: A qualitative study of the Family Card Programme in Bangladesh.

Public-Private Partnership in Transport Infrastructure: A comparative study of the Dhaka Elevated Expressway and Don Muang Tollway.

Leadership and Experience

Founder of BD English Learners, a free English-learning initiative supporting students’ communication and confidence. Other activities include student representation, educational support, campus ambassadorship and youth leadership.

Achievements and Training

University-level first place in the Youth for Human Rights International essay competition; training in qualitative research methodology, human rights, adolescent leadership, spoken English and professional development.
