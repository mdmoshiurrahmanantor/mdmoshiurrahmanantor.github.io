About Me

I study Public Administration at Bangladesh University of Professionals. I am interested in understanding how public institutions work, how government programmes affect people, and how public services can become more effective, inclusive and accountable.

Research Interests & Selected Projects

Digital Governance

Digital Transformation and Organizational Change in Bangladesh’s Public Sector

A qualitative study of E-Porcha implementation in land offices, focusing on service delivery, staff adaptation and organizational challenges.

Conceptual lens: ADKAR model with organizational and institutional context.

Human Rights

Law Enforcement and Human Rights in Bangladesh

A qualitative study examining law enforcement and human rights across two periods: 2009–2024 and the period after August 2024.

Focus: Accountability, legal safeguards, institutional practices and reform.

Social Protection & Gender

Women’s Participation in Social Protection Programs

A study of women’s participation in the Family Card Programme in Bangladesh, including access to benefits, decision-making and implementation challenges.

Conceptual lens: Naila Kabeer’s resources, agency and achievements framework.

Infrastructure & PPP

Comparative Study of Two Tollway Projects

A comparison of the Dhaka Elevated Expressway in Bangladesh and Don Muang Tollway in Thailand.

Comparison areas: Financing structure and risk allocation and management.

These entries describe selected academic research projects and work in progress. They are not presented as published journal articles.
